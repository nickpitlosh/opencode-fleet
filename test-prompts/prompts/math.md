# Domain: Basic Math Proficiency

Demonstrate mathematical proficiency by solving the following problems step-by-step, showing all work:

1. **Arithmetic & Number Theory**: Compute 2^1000 mod 13 using Fermat's little theorem. Show the reduction.
2. **Algebra**: Solve the system: x² + y² = 25, x + y = 7. Find all real and complex solutions.
3. **Geometry**: A regular dodecahedron has edge length 2. Compute its volume, surface angle defect, and circumscribed sphere radius.
4. **Probability**: In a game, you roll 5 dice. What's the probability of a full house? Compute exactly as a reduced fraction.
5. **Calculus**: Find the Taylor series of ln(1+x³) about x=0 to order x¹². Determine the radius of convergence.
6. **Linear Algebra**: Given matrix A = [[1,2,3],[4,5,6],[7,8,9]], find its rank, null space, and explain why it's singular using both determinant and row-reduction arguments.
7. **Combinatorics**: How many ways to distribute 20 identical balls into 5 distinct boxes where each box gets at least 2 balls?
8. **Proof**: Prove that √3 is irrational using a method different from the standard √2 proof.

Provide exact answers, not decimal approximations (unless specified). Show all reasoning.
